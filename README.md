The APK route exists because Play Store availability is regional. The trade-off is that Play gives you automated updates and Play Protect scanning; the APK gives you reach and speed, and you accept manual updating.

There's no "APK vs real app" quality difference — it's the same app from the same publisher, delivered two ways.

## Android: APK install, step by step

Roughly 3–5 minutes if your phone cooperates.

1. Open your phone browser (Chrome, Samsung Internet, whatever's default) and load Gate's download page from the link above.
2. Tap **Android APK app download**.
3. Tap **Download** and wait for the file to finish. Sizes change with every release, so don't judge the file by its size — judge it by where it came from.
4. Tap the installation prompt, then **Continue installation**.
5. When it finishes, choose **Done** or **Open**.

Two Android settings cause almost all failed installs:

**"Installation of apps from unknown sources is prohibited."** Go to Settings → Security → More security settings → Install apps from external sources, then allow it for your browser or download manager specifically. Some Chinese ROMs (Huawei/Honor, Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo) hide this behind slightly different paths but the wording is similar.

**Pure Mode on Huawei/Honor.** Settings → System & updates → Pure mode → Enhanced protection → turn it off. If the install still refuses after both steps, Gate's documented workaround is genuinely odd but works: switch on Airplane Mode to kill all networks, repeat the two settings, then install again.

After install, opening the app drops you on a short intro you can swipe past or skip.

## Problems people hit after installing

**The install keeps failing on Android.** It's almost always the unknown-sources permission or Pure Mode. The airplane-mode trick in the section above exists precisely because some ROMs re-block the permission when they detect a network connection.

**The app won't update.** On iOS, delete and reinstall. On Android, re-download the current APK and install over the top; your account isn't affected because nothing is stored locally.

**Reviews complain about update spam and freezing.** Scanning the Google Play reviews (the listing has roughly 88,900 of them) turns up a recurring theme: frequent small version updates with a notification every launch, plus reports of the home screen freezing for some users in a given build. Gate replies to these in the review thread and routes people to in-app support. It's worth reading recent reviews for your platform before you commit, especially if stability on your specific phone model matters.

**Customer support is slow when something goes wrong.** The negative App Store reviews are mostly withdrawal-related — pending transactions, requests for a transaction hash, and slow replies. Gate's own FAQ says withdrawal times depend on blockchain confirmation and typically run from minutes to a few hours, but if a transaction gets flagged for review, that timeline stops being useful. Keep your own records: withdrawal order numbers and hashes, taken from inside the app.

**Your country might be blocked.** Gate restricts services in some jurisdictions, and play stores enforce their own rules. Nothing in a download guide overrides local law.
